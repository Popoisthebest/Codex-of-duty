Evaluate:
- aim responsiveness
- movement-to-gun interaction
- ADS
- recoil/recovery
- hit/kill confirmation
- damage feedback
- reload state
- camera effects
- target readability
- engagement distance fit
- time-to-kill consistency if defined

Do not focus on weapon cosmetics unless they affect combat readability.

Report evidence and highest-impact defect.
