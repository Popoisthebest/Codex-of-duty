# Map Design Bar

The map should feel designed for repeated combat, not generated to meet dimensions.

## Required topology

At minimum:
- two team-side deployment/spawn regions
- central conflict region
- left flank route family
- right flank route family
- at least one indoor connector
- at least two loops that reconnect routes
- at least two useful elevation bands

## Zone identity

Each major zone should have at least two of:
- distinctive silhouette
- material palette variation
- landmark prop/structure
- light/color temperature difference
- different engagement distance
- unique route topology

A player should be able to communicate "where" a fight happened.

## Cover rhythm

Avoid:
- random prop spam
- perfectly repeated cover spacing
- long empty rectangles
- full-height cover everywhere
- no-cover sightlines from spawn to spawn

Provide:
- approach cover
- fighting cover
- transition cover
- occasional exposed risk/reward crossing

## Sightlines

Mix:
- close interior
- medium street/courtyard
- selective long sightline

Long sightlines need:
- interruptions
- flank counters
- spawn safety consideration

## Loops

A route that dead-ends without tactical payoff is not a meaningful alternative.

Important areas should have more than one escape/entry path where appropriate.

## Spawn design

Spawn zones need depth and exits.

A good spawn:
- is not directly visible from common enemy positions
- has multiple exit choices
- can route into different zones
- does not force repeated same encounter

## Scale

Dimensions alone do not matter.

A 120x120 map full of dead space can be worse than an 80x90 dense, interconnected map.

Use the numeric gate only as a floor.
